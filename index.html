07THE NEXT CHAPTER

The next leap
is not more AI.
It’s more everyday.

The opportunity is to make AI useful beyond the power user: agents that act, creative tools with a distinct edge, and business models that let people start free and pay when value shows up.

Subscriptions will stay. Ads and transactions could make the magic accessible to more than the 4.5% already paying for a leading assistant.
